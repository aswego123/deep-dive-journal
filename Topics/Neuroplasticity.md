# Neuroplasticity

Neuro = Nervous System (Brain)
Plasticity = Moldable

Our brain is capable of learning and growing at any age
## Sub-branches


## 🪝 The Hook

> The brain isn't a fixed machine that just declines after childhood — it physically rewires itself based on what you repeatedly do, think, and pay attention to, well into old age.

## ⚙️ Core Mechanism

> Neurons that fire together wire together. Repeated activity strengthens synaptic connections; unused ones get pruned. This happens through structural changes (new dendritic spines, myelination) not just chemical ones.

## 💡 So What

> If I want a skill or mindset to become automatic, the lever isn't willpower — it's repetition + focused attention over time. Deliberate, spaced practice matters more than intensity.

## ❓ Open Question

> How much plasticity is actually lost with age vs. how much is just reduced *opportunity* for novel, effortful practice?
